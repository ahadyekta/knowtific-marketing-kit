# Client discovery questions

Use the sections that fit. Ask the money questions early: they decide whether ads can work.

## Business and offer
- What services or products make you the most money? Which do you want more of?
- What does a typical job or order cost the customer? What's the gross profit on it?
- What's your entry product or service, and what do customers buy next?
- How often do customers come back or refer others? What's a customer worth over a year or two?
- What's your service area? Where do you not want to travel?
- How much more work can you handle per week right now?
- Do you offer any guarantee, extras or packages?

## Customers and insights
- Describe your best recent customer. Why did they pick you?
- Who do you not want as a customer?
- What was happening in customers' lives when they started looking for you?
- What do customers usually say when they're happy? (Pull real review wording.)
- What questions or worries come up before people book? What almost stops them?
- Why do people choose a competitor instead of you?
- Are your customers price-driven, quality-driven, or buying a premium experience? (Which buyer type?)
- What do your customers pay attention to through the year (seasons, events, school terms, EOFY)?

## Competition and positioning
- Who are your top 3 competitors? What do they do well and badly?
- What do you do that they don't, or won't?
- If you could be known for one word, what would it be?
- When someone says "Oh, like [competitor]?", what do you answer?

## Brand and identity (for rebrands and redesigns)
- What do you love and hate about your current name, logo, colours and website?
- Which brands (any industry) do you admire the look and feel of? Why?
- What must stay (brand equity: recognisable colours, vehicle wraps, signage, name recognition)?
- How do you want people to feel when they see you?

## Proof and assets
- Google reviews count and rating? Video testimonials? (Health: check AHPRA rules.)
- Before/after photos, job site photos, case studies?
- Licences, certifications, awards, years in business, number of jobs done?
- Who is willing to be on camera? Which locations look good on camera?
- Do any customers or partners make content or have audiences?

## Sales process
- What happens after someone enquires? Who responds, how fast, and how?
- What share of enquiries become jobs (close rate)? What share of quotes are accepted?
- How many times do you follow up a quote?
- Do you record sales calls? Could we get notes?
- Which CRM or job management tool do you use (ServiceM8, Tradify, Fergus, Simpro, HubSpot, other)?
- What do you send people before and after a quote or consultation?

## Current marketing
- Where do your leads come from today, roughly by percentage?
- What have you tried before that didn't work, and why do you think it failed?
- Current ad spend, cost per lead, and who manages it?
- Website: who built it, can we change it, is tracking installed (GA4, Meta pixel, Google Ads conversions, call tracking)?
- Social accounts, who posts, how often, what's worked?
- Email/SMS list size and what you send?
- Google Business Profile: who manages it?

## Partners, events, retail
- Do you sell through retailers, resellers or partners?
- Which events, trade shows or markets do you attend?
- Which other businesses share your customers?

## Seasonality and goals
- Busy and quiet months?
- Revenue or job-count goal for the next 3 and 12 months?
- Monthly budget you're comfortable testing with, and for how long?
