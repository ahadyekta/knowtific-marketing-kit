# Client discovery questions

Use the sections that fit. Ask the money questions early; they decide whether ads can work.

## Business and offer
- What services make you the most money? Which do you want more of?
- What does a typical job cost the customer? What's the gross profit on it?
- How often do customers come back or refer others?
- What's your service area? Where do you not want to travel?
- How much more work can you handle per week right now?

## Customers
- Describe your best recent customer. Why did they pick you?
- Who do you not want as a customer?
- What do customers usually say when they're happy? (Pull real review wording.)
- What questions or worries come up before people book?
- Why do people choose a competitor instead of you?

## Competition and positioning
- Who are your top 3 competitors? What do they do well and badly?
- What do you do that they don't, or won't?
- If you could be known for one word, what would it be?

## Proof and assets
- Google reviews count and rating? Any video testimonials? (Health: check AHPRA rules.)
- Before/after photos, job site photos, case studies?
- Licences, certifications, awards, years in business, number of jobs done?
- Who is willing to be on camera? Which locations look good on camera?

## Sales process
- What happens after someone enquires? Who calls them, and how fast?
- What share of enquiries become jobs (close rate)?
- What share of quotes are accepted?
- Do you use a CRM or job management tool (ServiceM8, Tradify, Fergus, Simpro, other)?
- Do you follow up quotes? How many times?

## Current marketing
- Where do your leads come from today, roughly by percentage?
- What have you tried before that didn't work, and why do you think it failed?
- Current ad spend, cost per lead, and who manages it?
- Website: who built it, can we change it, is tracking installed (GA4, Meta pixel, Google Ads conversions)?
- Social accounts and who posts?

## Seasonality and goals
- Busy and quiet months?
- Revenue or job-count goal for the next 3 and 12 months?
- Monthly budget you're comfortable testing with, and for how long?
