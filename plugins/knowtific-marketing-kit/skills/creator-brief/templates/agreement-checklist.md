# Creator agreement checklist

Confirm in writing (email is fine for small deals; a contract for larger ones):

- [ ] **Deliverables**: number of posts/videos, platforms, lengths, versions (hooks, endings)
- [ ] **Raw files**: unedited footage, no music or text, delivered via link
- [ ] **Post on their account**: yes/no, date, caption approval
- [ ] **Partnership ad code** (Meta) / Spark Ads code (TikTok): provided, valid for how long
- [ ] **Usage rights**: where (paid ads, website, email, organic reposts) and how long (e.g. 3 months of paid ads)
- [ ] **Edits allowed**: can we cut, recaption, add subtitles?
- [ ] **Exclusivity** (if any): competitors, category, duration
- [ ] **Price**: base fee + ad usage fee (+ product/gift value), payment terms, ABN/invoice, GST
- [ ] **Disclosure**: paid partnership label/#ad
- [ ] **Claims**: what they can and can't say (provable claims only; health and TGA rules)
- [ ] **Approval and revisions**: how many rounds, turnaround
- [ ] **Deadline** and what happens if late
- [ ] **Music**: platform-licensed audio only for ads
- [ ] **Model releases** for anyone else who appears (and minors need guardian consent)
