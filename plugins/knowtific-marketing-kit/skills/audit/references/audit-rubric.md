# Audit rubric

Score each area 0–5. Use the descriptions as anchors; half points are fine.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Missing or broken |
| 1 | Exists but generic or mostly broken |
| 2 | Basic, several clear gaps |
| 3 | Solid basics, some gaps |
| 4 | Strong, minor improvements |
| 5 | Excellent, a competitive advantage |

## 1. Positioning and messaging
- 5: a clear, specific positioning statement; homepage headline states it; passes the shelf and elevator tests; AI-legible; consistent across site, GBP, social and ads
- 2: some specifics but mostly generic claims ("quality, reliable, friendly")
- 0: no discernible difference from competitors
Check: headline, about page, GBP description, bios, ads copy.

## 2. Offer and lead magnets
- 5: a specific offer statement, clear packages or ranges, a genuine guarantee or risk reducer, a useful lead magnet for not-ready buyers, an entry offer and next offers
- 2: "contact us for a quote" only
Check: services pages, pricing signals, CTAs, opt-ins.

## 3. Website and landing pages
- 5: dedicated pages per core service/campaign following a proven structure; fast on mobile; message match with ads; proof and FAQ; strong CTA repeated; click-to-call
- 2: one generic page for everything; weak proof; slow
Use the landing-page-reviewer score as input (÷20).

## 4. Funnel and follow-up
- 5: response within 5 minutes; instant acknowledgement; thank-you page with next steps; booking confirmation/preparation page; multi-touch follow-up with reasons; close assets; stop-on-reply automation
- 2: replies within a day or two; one follow-up at most
- 0: leads go to an unmonitored inbox
Test: submit a test enquiry (with permission) or ask the owner.

## 5. Tracking and measurement
- 5: GA4 key events, Meta pixel + Conversions API, Google Ads conversions incl. calls, UTMs, CRM source tracking, someone reviews numbers weekly
- 2: GA4 installed but no conversions
- 0: no tracking

## 6. SEO and Google Business Profile
- 5: GBP fully optimised (categories, services, photos, posts, Q&A); service pages for core terms; top 3 for main local terms; consistent NAP; schema; healthy Core Web Vitals; Search Console set up
- 2: GBP claimed but thin; few service pages; not ranking
Use DataForSEO or Search Console data if available.

## 7. Reviews and reputation
- 5: strong rating, steady flow of recent reviews, replies to every review, review request built into the job-complete flow
- 2: few or old reviews, no replies
Check: Google, Facebook, industry sites; competitors' counts for comparison.

## 8. Organic social
- 5: one focused primary platform, a person or lens delivering expertise, 3–5 pillars reviewed monthly, consistent cadence, replies to comments/DMs, cross-posting, results reported
- 2: sporadic product or job photos, no strategy
Check: last 30 posts, views, engagement, bio CTA.

## 9. Paid ads
- 5: economics known (target CPL/CPA); clean structure (one concept per ad set); weekly testing of new creative; kill/scale rules; good naming; tracking verified; search terms and negatives managed
- 2: running boosted posts or a single always-on campaign with no testing
- n/a if not running ads (score the readiness instead)

## 10. Email and SMS
- 5: core flows live (welcome/enquiry, quote follow-up, review request, rebook/win-back); regular campaigns mixing types; plain-text founder emails; segments; compliance
- 2: occasional newsletters only
- 0: no list

## 11. Creators, partners and events
- 5: recurring creators or ambassadors, partner co-promotion, seeding or experience program, events with goals, enabled content
- 1–2: none or ad hoc
(Score relative to what suits the business; a sole trader isn't expected to run a creator pyramid.)

## 12. Sales support and process
- 5: collateral by stage (first email, call deck, forwardable pack, case studies, FAQ), call notes reviewed for objections, triage of social leads, CRM used consistently
- 2: quotes sent with no supporting material

## 13. Compliance
- 5: claims provable; health rules followed; licence numbers shown; disclosures; privacy policy; messaging consent and unsubscribes
- Any serious breach (clinical testimonials for AHPRA professions, fake reviews, no unsubscribe) caps the score at 1 and goes in the top fixes

## 14. Brand identity and design
- 5: distinctive, consistent identity across site, vehicles, signage, social; looks premium for its tier; photography of real work and people
- 2: dated or inconsistent; stock photos; weak logo
