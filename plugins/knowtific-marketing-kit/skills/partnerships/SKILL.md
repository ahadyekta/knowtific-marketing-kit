# Partnerships, events and retail

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/marketing-coach/references/partners-events-retail.md` and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/marketing-coach/references/campaigns.md` section 6.

## Inputs
- `marketing/brand.md`, `brand-world.md` (friends, calendar), `offer.md`, `economics.md`.
- Existing partners, suppliers, referral sources, retailers/resellers; events attended; budget.

## Mode 1: Partners and collaborations
1. List 10 partner candidates who share the client's customers but don't compete (complementary trades, suppliers, local businesses, associations, creators with audiences). For each: why them, what's in it for them, the first ask.
2. Pick the best 3 and design the collaboration: idea (joint offer, co-created product/package, joint event, content swap, referral arrangement), who pays/does what/gets what, assets each side makes, timeline, how results are tracked.
3. A collaboration calendar that fills gaps in the client's release schedule.
4. Outreach message for each.
5. Licensing (if relevant): what IP would resonate with the audience and the basic economics.

## Mode 2: Event plan
1. Choose the event type and why (selling event, B2B trade show, appreciation/VIP event, piggyback, local meetup, workshop).
2. Goal and break-even: total cost → required direct sales, orders or qualified leads.
3. Before: meeting booking plan (lists, LinkedIn and email outreach, invitations to customers), promotion, staffing (an extroverted seller), stock and payment setup.
4. On the day: booth or venue plan designed for content (what people will film), lead capture (QR to a form, tablet), offer, the night-before filming slot.
5. After: same-week follow-up sequence, content recap, results vs goal.

## Mode 3: Retail and resellers
1. The pitch to get stocked: data (regional traffic and search demand), marketing support (dedicated creators, campaigns), the shelf gap filled, margins and terms.
2. Merchandising: shelf-ready packaging, aisle interrupters, displays, campaign swaps.
3. Staff training: what every floor salesperson must know, training format (online, in-person, field visits), incentives.
4. Partner/reseller content network: shared content folder, launch date notice, collab posts with the best partners, regional paid spend to partners.
5. Collab posts and newsletter features with retailers.

## Output
Save to `marketing/campaigns/partnerships-<topic>.md`. In chat: the top 3 opportunities with the first action for each.
