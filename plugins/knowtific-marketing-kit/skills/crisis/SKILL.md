# Crisis response

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/marketing-coach/references/pr-and-narrative.md` section 2 and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/marketing-coach/references/australia-compliance.md`.

## 1. Facts first
Ask (in one message) or extract: what happened, when, where it's posted, how far it's spread (views, shares, comments, media), what's true and what isn't, who's affected, what the business has already said or done, any legal or regulatory angle (health complaints, safety, discrimination, defamation).

## 2. Options
Lay out 3–5 options, such as:
1. Public apology and fix
2. Calm factual explanation, standing firm
3. Private contact to resolve, with a brief public reply
4. Stay quiet and monitor (small, unfair complaints that aren't spreading)
5. Combination

## 3. Role-play reactions
For each option, predict reactions from: the complainant, their friends/commenters, existing customers, potential customers reading later, staff, and critics who might escalate. What could people get angry about? What could backfire? What counterbalance can the business create (a fix, a policy change, a gesture)?

## 4. Recommend and draft
- The recommended option and why.
- **Public reply** (short, calm, human, no arguing, no private details, an offer to resolve offline).
- **Private message or call script.**
- Internal note for staff (what to say if asked).
- If it's a review: whether it breaches the platform's policies (fake, conflict of interest, off-topic) and how to flag it. Don't request removal of genuine negative reviews just because they're negative.

## 5. Follow-up
Root-cause fix, what to tell people once fixed, monitoring plan (who checks, how often, for how long), and whether to address it in an email or post later.

## Rules
- Speed matters, but accuracy matters more: never state anything untrue.
- Never reveal customer personal information or health details.
- Don't delete legitimate criticism.
- Recommend legal advice for defamation, discrimination, safety incidents or regulator involvement; health practitioners should consider their AHPRA obligations and professional indemnity insurer.
- Save the plan to `marketing/reports/crisis-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
